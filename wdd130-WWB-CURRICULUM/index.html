Wetaskiwin, Alberta

Wetaskiwin is a city located in Alberta, Canada, approximately 70 kilometers south of Edmonton. The name Wetaskiwin comes from the Cree word "wītaskiwinihk," meaning "the hills where peace was made." The city is known for its rich history and cultural heritage, including the Reynolds-Alberta Museum, which celebrates technological advancements in transportation, industry, and agriculture. Wetaskiwin is also famous for its slogan "Cars cost less in Wetaskiwin," which has been a staple in Western Canada since the mid-1970s.
